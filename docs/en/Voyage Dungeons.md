A separate world with dungeons, custom blocks and items, its own mobs, and a boss. The goal: reach this dimension, find a dungeon, clear the maze, kill the boss, and claim a unique reward.

## How to reach the dimension

1. Craft **9 Particle Blocks** (recipe: 4 prismarine + 3 crying obsidian + 2 sponge on a crafting table).
2. Build a frame **4 wide × 5 tall** out of them (just like a Nether portal) — 10 blocks around the perimeter, empty inside.
3. Light it with flint and steel from inside the frame.

Important: the portal only works after an admin has opened the dimension with a command. If it isn't working, ask an admin.

To get back out of the dimension, step into any portal you've built, or return to the spot you came down from (teleporting through the same portal works both ways).

## The "Crystal Mines" dimension itself

- Eternal night, no rain, a purple-and-blue palette.
- The surface is dark grass and dirt, with crystal "towers" of glowing veins growing on every side.
- Underground is all stone — replaced with bluish **crystal stone**.
- No natural mobs (sheep, zombies, and the rest don't spawn). The only danger is the dungeons.

### What spawns underground

All the usual ores are here — coal, iron, copper, gold, redstone, lapis, diamond. Same depths as the Overworld.
Plus one new ore — **Voyage Ore** (orange). It's found at the same depths as diamond, but twice as rare. It drops a **Voyage Ingot**.

## Dungeons

The dimension's main attraction. Finding one isn't hard — they generate roughly every 300 blocks in any direction. From the outside you'll spot a small stone portal pad with a hole leading down and a ladder.

### What's inside

Below the ladder is an entrance room. From there, doors lead into the maze:

- **5 to 9 mob rooms** along the main path
- Sometimes **side branches** that dead-end into an extra room with a chest (up to 5 branches)
- Every room has a **spawner** on the floor (it spawns mobs while you're nearby)
- A **closed chest** in the corner of every room
- At the end of the main path — a **boss room** with the boss
- Behind the boss — a **double chest** with better loot

### Sealed chests

While the dungeon boss is alive, **every chest in the dungeon is locked**. You'll get the message "Chest sealed — kill the dungeon boss to open it." This stops you from running past the mobs straight to the loot.

Once the boss is dead, all the chests open. An opened chest stays open forever (even if the boss respawns 4 hours later).

### Respawn

The dungeon mobs and boss come back automatically **4 real-time hours** after death. You can farm it.

## Dungeon mobs

### Fatling

Big, slow, fat. Soaks up a lot of damage before it dies. Its most dangerous trick is the **dash-jump**: if you're 10–20 blocks away, it can leap right onto your head (once every 5 seconds). On landing it deals area damage and a shockwave. Best to stay right next to it or more than 20 blocks away.
- **Health:** 120
- **Melee damage:** 6
- **XP:** 10

### Crusher

Small, fast, bitey. Less HP, but quicker and more dangerous in a pack.
- **Health:** 50
- **Damage:** 6
- **XP:** 10

### Grim Seraph (boss)

A flying boss at the end of every dungeon. Huge, proud, with a blue boss health bar across the top of the screen.

**What it can do:**

- Flies (can't be knocked down or pushed back)
- Very tough armor — a regular iron sword barely scratches it
- **Spells**: it periodically enters a casting stance — during these moments it's **invulnerable**, so you have to wait them out
- Applies the **"Seraph's Freeze"** debuff (slowness + locks your actions)
- Throws magic orbs

**Stats:**
- **Health:** 1500
- **Armor:** 20
- **Contact damage:** 4
- **XP per kill:** 50 levels

**Drop:** exactly **1 random organ** (one of four — Eye of the Void / Heart of the Abyss / Athlete's Thigh / Dragon's Heel). To assemble the full armor set you need to kill the boss at least 4 times.

## Rewards and items

### From the dungeon chests

In the regular room chests you can find:

**Common:**

- Iron ingots (3–8)
- Crystal glass (6–32 blocks) — a decorative blue block
- Gold ingots (3–16)

**Uncommon:**

- Enchanted books (random enchantment, around level ~30 — usually good)
- Particle Blocks (1–3 — the stuff the portal is made of)
- Golden apples (1–4)
- Diamonds (1–6)

**Rare:**

- Netherite ingot (1–3)
- Voyage Customs items

### From the boss chest (double)

Everything the regular chests have, plus **sharply boosted rare-drop chances** and extra exclusives:

- **Netherite ingots** (triple the chance)
- **Crystal Miner's Pickaxe**
- **Amulet of Might**
- **Amulet of Excavation**
- **Voyage Customs items**
- **A random boss organ** (same as from the Seraph itself, without having to kill it)
- **Voyage Ingot** (1–3)

## Special items

### Crystal Miner's Pickaxe

A netherite-tier pickaxe, twice as durable as a regular netherite one, dealing 1 extra damage.

**Special trick:** hold **Shift + right-click** on any block to automatically place a torch from your inventory on the targeted block. Handy when digging tunnels.

### Amulet of Might

Toggle on/off with a right-click. It works while it's switched on and sitting in your inventory.

When **on** AND you're holding a **sword** in your main hand, it passively grants **+40% damage**. Put the sword away and the bonus disappears (no need to re-toggle the amulet).

The activation/deactivation chime shows above the inventory.

### Amulet of Excavation

Toggle on/off with a right-click. It works while it's switched on and sitting in your inventory.

When **on** AND you're holding a **pickaxe** in your main hand, breaking any block **automatically breaks the block ABOVE it too** (with the same drops). Essentially you mine 2 blocks tall at once, which is handy for tunnels.

## The "Abyss Armor" set (crafted from boss drops)

All 4 pieces are made on a crafting table from Voyage Ingots + 1 organ in the center. Stylistically they look like "abyss armor" — purple-and-black with accents. **Key restriction:** you can wear **only ONE Abyss piece at a time**. Try to put on a second and the first automatically pops back into your inventory. This isn't a bug — it's a balance feature: you have to choose which ability to run (dark vision, thorns, speed, or parkour).

|Piece|Organ in center|What it gives|
|---|---|---|
|**Abyss Helmet**|Eye of the Void|Permanent **night vision** while worn|
|**Abyss Chestplate**|Heart of the Abyss|**Thorns V** — the attacker takes 5 damage when they hit you|
|**Abyss Leggings**|Athlete's Thigh|**Double run speed** — +40% while walking, +200% while sprinting|
|**Abyss Boots**|Dragon's Heel|**Immunity to fall damage** from any height + automatic 1-block step-up (like a horse)|
