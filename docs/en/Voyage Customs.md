## Cosmetic Accessories (5)


### 1. Wanderer's Ring

When you walk or fly, a beautiful glowing trail follows behind you. The trail color is fixed (soft blue).

### 2. RGB Ring

Same as the Wanderer's Ring, but you pick the trail color yourself. Open your inventory and a "Trail Colour" button appears on the left — choose any color you like. If you wear both rings at once, RGB overrides the regular trail color.

### 3. Divine Nimbus

A glowing golden halo disc floats above your head. It bobs smoothly up and down and slowly spins. It glows even in the dark. No bonuses — just looks great.

### 4. Gravedigger's Hat

**Bonus (shown in gold in the tooltip):** +25% to your mob-kill count in "Killer Mob" events.

**Appearance:** a black top hat sits on your head and turns with it.

## Belts (11 total)

Every belt has one **main** effect (baked into the belt itself) plus two side effects that **drop randomly from mobs in the crystal dimension**.

**Strength:**

- Main effect — full strength
- Side effect — one third of the strength

### What the belts do

**Damage Belt** — increases your weapon damage. Main +25%, side +7.5%.

**Mining Belt** — Haste II while worn. Side effect — Haste I. Break blocks faster with any tool.

**Speed Belt** — Speed II / Speed I. Only affects walking and running, not flying.

**Luck Belt** — _works like a Fortune enchantment on your pickaxe, but you don't need to enchant the pickaxe itself._ Main grants Fortune III, side grants Fortune I. Works with any pickaxe (vanilla or modded) and any block — wherever Fortune normally yields extra drops, now it does for you too.

**Protection Belt** — Resistance II / Resistance I. Reduces all incoming damage.

**Regeneration Belt** — Regeneration II / I plus extra health: **+10 HP (5 hearts) per main belt**, **+4 HP (2 hearts) per side effect**.

**Trader Belt** — Hero of the Village. Villagers give discounts and better quest rewards. Stacks on top of the vanilla raid buff.

**Collector Belt:**

- **Extra inventory** — while the belt is worn, an extra row of 9 personal slots appears at the bottom of your inventory. Take the belt off and the row disappears, but everything stored inside is saved and comes back when you put it on again.
- **Magnet** — a separate **X** key (rebindable). Press it to turn the magnet on, press again to turn it off. While on, all nearby dropped items are pulled toward you. Radius: **10 blocks** for the main effect, **6 blocks** for the side effect. The pull is fast — items get picked up instantly.

**Looter Belt** — killed mobs drop more loot. **40% chance** to double each dropped item (main), **12%** (side). Capped at 80% per drop.

**Night Vision Belt:**

- Permanent Night Vision — you see in the dark, and the effect never fades.
- **Main:** hostile mobs can't target you at all. Zombies, skeletons, spiders, creepers, endermen — none of them aggro. None.
- **Side:** only Night Vision + a periodic aggro reset on mobs within 8 blocks. Between resets, mobs may get a hit in — so it isn't full protection.
- _Special case:_ very smart mobs (Warden, Piglin Brute) use a different aggro mechanic — their protection only works through the periodic reset, like the side effect.

**Frost Belt:**

- **Active ability on the Z key (rebindable).** Freezes hostile mobs around you:
    - **Main:** **15-block** radius, **3.5 s** freeze, **6 s** cooldown, very strong slowdown.
    - **Side:** **7-block** radius, **1.5 s** freeze, **12 s** cooldown, medium slowdown.
- **Passive:** constantly removes Slowness and Mining Fatigue from you.
- The freeze doesn't affect other players — this isn't a PvP item.

## How to get belts

- **Drop chance: 15%** from a killed boss.
- **Who drops them:** mobs from the crystal dimension mod — Crusher, Fatling, Grim Seraph.
- **What exactly drops:** a random belt out of the 11, with both side effects randomized as well.

|**X**|Toggle the Collector Belt magnet|

Both keys can be rebound under _Options → Controls → Voyage Customs_.
