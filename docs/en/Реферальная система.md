
The referral system is a program under which someone who invites another player is entitled to a reward for bringing players into the season. The system benefits both the project and its distributors: the project gains new players, and the distributor gets a reward matching their contribution.
If an invited player buys a pass to take part in the prize-pool draw, both the distributor and the invited player are entitled to the bonus set out in this article.

To make a recruited player your referral, you need to tell the prospective player to enter your in-game nickname when filling out the pass-purchase form. A player counts as your referral and qualifies you for bonuses strictly up until they buy a pass. After a pass is bought independently, the player is considered individually invited and no one can be credited with bonuses for their play.

Both players receive the reward. Currently the reward is a +0.05x | +5% increase to the point-earning coefficient for the invited player and the distributor alike. Plus a unique customization item that sets you apart from other players.
The maximum possible increase from inviting referrals is +0.25x | 25% for a distributor with a base-level pass.
