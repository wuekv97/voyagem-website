Points are the core system built to track player activity by awarding points for various actions. This chapter covers where points come from, when coefficients drop, and what limits apply to each method.
All the ratios listed below are experimental.
#### 🪨 **Ore** (`OrePointsHandler`)

| Ore             | Points | Award Chance |
| --------------- | ------ | ------------ |
| Ancient Debris  | 16.67  | 36%          |
| Diamond Ore     | 10.00  | 18%          |
| Emerald Ore     | 10.00  | 15%          |
| Gold Ore        | 3.33   | 15%          |
- **Formula:** `base × oreCoefficient × global × dimension × player`
- **Coefficient drop:** −10% every **16 broken blocks** (each ore has its own counter)
- **Minimum coefficient:** 0.30
- **Not awarded** if the block was placed by a player (anti-farm)
### 🎣 **Fishing** (`FishingPointsHandler`)

|Parameter|Value|
|---|---|
|**Base**|1 point|
|**Chance**|100% (for every successful catch of any kind)|
|**Formula**|`1 × global × dimension × player`|
|**Limit**|**1000 accumulated points → hard stop**|
|**Reset**|`/points admin resetcoefficients <nick>`|

- There's no dynamic coefficient drop — it just stops after 1000
- Triggered by a `tick` detector: fish bites + player successfully reels in → +1
### ⚔️ **Mobs** (`MobPointsHandler`)

Base formula: `(maxHealth × 0.03) + (attackDamage × 0.04)`, then it depends on the type:
Take a zombie with 20 health and 4 attack damage as an example. It awards 20×0.03 + 4×0.04 = 0.76 per mob before the coefficient starts dropping.
Passive mobs, however, get a multiplier that's 3× smaller, so a chicken with 4 HP works out to: 4×0.01 = 0.04 per chicken's life.

|Category|Multiplier|Coefficient Drop|Minimum|
|---|---|---|---|
|**Bosses** (Ender Dragon, Wither)|**× 10**|−20% **per kill**|0.20|
|**Hostile** (Zombie, Skeleton, Creeper, etc.)|× 1|−20% every **40 kills**|0.20|
|**Neutral** _(only while passive)_|÷ 2|−20% every **40 kills**|0.20|
|**Passive** (cow, sheep, chicken, etc.)|÷ 3|−20% every **40 kills**|0.20|

**Neutral mobs** (only counted if they're NOT aggroed at the moment of the kill): Bee, Enderman, Iron Golem, Llama, Piglin, Polar Bear, Spider, Trader Llama, Wolf, Zombified Piglin. If these same mobs are aggressive, they count as hostile.

**Each mob species** has its own counter and coefficient (i.e. 40 zombies and 40 skeletons = two independent counts).
### 🌐 **Global multipliers** (always applied)

| Multiplier                                          | Default | Can change during the season?                          |
| --------------------------------------------------- | ------- | ------------------------------------------------------ |
| `Base coefficient`                                  | 1.0     | No                                                     |
| `Overworld multiplier`                              | 1.0     | Yes                                                   |
| `The Nether / The End / Voyage multiplier`          | 2.0     | Yes                                                   |
| `Unique player multiplier`                          | 1.0     | Yes                                                   |
| `Referral multiplier (referral system)`             | +0.05   | Yes — name your inviter's nick at purchase to claim it |
### 🔄 **Coefficient reset**

Coefficients reset automatically every day at 22:00 Kyiv time.
After the reset you can go back to farming points at full rate, with no reduced coefficients — right up until you hit a reduced coefficient again.
### 🏆 **Achievements**

On top of routine farming, points are awarded for completing achievements (advancements) from **any branch** — vanilla (Adventure, Nether, The End, Husbandry, etc.) and modded, including **Voyage Dungeons**. Detection works at the engine level, so any real achievement that shows up in the "Achievements" window counts.

|Who completed it|Points|Announcement|
|---|---|---|
|**First** player on the server to earn the achievement|**+100**|Globally in chat: `★ <nick> was FIRST to earn "<achievement>"`|
|All **subsequent** players for the same achievement|**+50**|Privately to the player, no server-wide notice|

- **Formula:** a fixed amount, **no coefficients** — world, personal, and referral multipliers have no effect on achievements. +100 and +50 are awarded exactly as is.
- **"First" is counted once** server-wide for each achievement and persists across restarts — even if the first finder goes offline, the bonus stays theirs, and everyone else already gets +50.
- **Anti-abuse:** each player earns points for the same achievement only once. Re-awarding it (e.g. via an admin revoking and re-granting) yields no points.
- **Not counted:** hidden "recipe achievements" (craft unlocks) — only real, displayed achievements.
- **No limits or resets:** unlike ore, fishing, and mobs, achievements have no decaying coefficients and no cap — each achievement awards points exactly once, forever.
