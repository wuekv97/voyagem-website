The season starts on 10.06.2026 and runs for 10 days. When the season ends, a payout calculation is run that factors in each player's points total as well as the overall prize pool formed from the number of passes purchased and all items bought on the site.
On top of that, the project's creator will make a one-time contribution of $100 to the pool; if player interest is high, this contribution may be increased.
Consider a scenario where 10 passes were purchased over the season's 10 days and players farmed 15,000 points. Divide the point count (15,000) by the amount of money in the pool (say, $200) and you get that every 75 points equals $1 / ₴44 / ₽71.
A hypothetical Pete with his 1,000 points would receive $13.
The project is not commercial for the first season. All invested funds were contributed purely out of the creator's personal interest.
